RUN IT FROM YOUR PHONE

Message your station from anywhere. The work happens at home.

PLAIN ENGLISH~3 MINNEEDS: A CHANNEL ACCOUNT

01What a channel is

A channel is a doorway from a chat app straight into your station.

Connect one and a message you send from your phone walks in through the front door, rides the line like any other job, and the finished work comes back to the same chat. Your station runs on your computer at home — the channel is just the doorway.

StarNet ships doorways for Telegram, Discord, Slack, Matrix and Signal. Pick whichever app you already use.

02Hook one up

  1. Open TOOLSETS

    Find the channel you want and connect it. Each one walks you through its own short setup.

    each channel has its own card
  2. Prove it’s you

    The first message pairs your account as the owner. Only the owner can give the station work — strangers who find the bot get nothing.

    one-time pairing
  3. Send it a job

    Message it like you’d message a person. Watch the station: a box comes through the front door.

    the box is your message

03Staying in control

Only you

Your account is paired as the owner. Nobody else can command your station through the channel.

E-STOP works remotely

Send the stop command from your phone and everything running halts, mid-job, right then.

Budgets still bind

A job from your phone obeys the same spending caps as one started at the keyboard.
