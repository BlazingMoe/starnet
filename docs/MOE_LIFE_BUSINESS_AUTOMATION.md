# Moe: life and business automation

Recorded 2026-10-02 from the operator's explicit product direction. This is a living delivery plan, not evidence that accounts are connected or businesses are running.

## Product goal

Moe is the operator's versatile central personal system for everyday life, learning, research, creative work, software, documents, data, communication, planning and business. The operator clarified that the following three jobs are examples of the breadth desired, not a restriction to three industries or a fixed delivery sequence:

- A YouTube channel that researches an audience, produces original useful videos, edits them, publishes them and learns from real results with minimal routine intervention.
- An Etsy operation that researches demand and target audiences, develops eligible products, prepares and manages listings, fulfils orders and reconciles results.
- Trading through a supported broker with explicit strategy, exposure limits, execution reconciliation and emergency stop, initially in simulation.

The general product requirement is a common goal-to-result execution path for varied assignments: understand, plan, choose existing tools and skills, execute, persist, recover, verify and deliver. Extend domains through compatible integrations and reusable recipes on the same runtime. Evaluate new work by actual need and usefulness across domains as well as complete outcomes. Support depends on available tools, account access and permissions; the examples do not imply universal capability.

Use scoped standing authorizations to remove repetitive approvals where the existing policy supports them. Initial account consent, policy setup and exceptional decisions remain necessary. This product direction does not itself authorize live trades, public uploads, customer messages, purchases or other financial commitments. No promise of revenue, investment returns or permanent operation without supervision is made.

## Example delivery paths and acceptance

General decision workflow: `decision-dossier` composes the existing research, source-triangulation and review skills with web/filesystem gear. Its launch form takes the decision, constraints and workspace folder. It requests a source ledger, comparable CSV and recommendation, with read-back verification and saved progress. The recipe is available across desktop and website catalogues; catalogue/launch tests are not evidence that an actual research dossier has been generated. The shared harness now exercises search/fetch, a host-receipt-validated `sources.md`, the other three artifacts, read-backs and cold checkpoint reconstruction through scripted model/web fixtures. A blocked entry records a host-observed transport outcome, not a fetched source. The validator proves required fields and same-run receipt references only; it does not establish claim support or truth. Display URLs omit credentials/query/fragment and are not exact resource identities; the excerpt digest covers the redacted web_fetch excerpt before the registry output cap, while post-fetch truncation is separately marked. Publication dates remain model-reported. A bounded live Codex run has fetched two primary sources and saved/read back all four files; follow-up review labeled inferences. Those initial runs ended at an iteration ceiling and a provider usage limit respectively. On 2026-10-06 a separate read-only review was interrupted, recovered through the existing journal/API after a real sidecar restart, and completed with all four file hashes unchanged; the original failed runs retain their status. This accepts the exercised API read-only recovery path, not browser interaction, interrupted writes or unattended operation. The recipe requires decisive source passages beyond truncated previews and distinguishes syntax facts from usability/recovery judgments. The next step is actual launch/consent UI acceptance and a bounded attended routine; do not repeat the now-successful read-only recovery rehearsal without a relevant change.

Journal restart safety: the cold-writer adoption principle from upstream `3ae8e4405` is integrated into the existing journal. A new instance reads and validates the durable chain and run identity before appending; a complete final record without a newline is separated durably. Torn or invalid records and read failures block cold appends without altering the original bytes. The parser rejects invalid or mixed run identities even when individual hashes validate, and direct inspection binds content to the requested run. Recovery scans and failed writes invalidate cached chain positions, so later appends revalidate disk even in the same process. Tests cover restart, missing newline, identity mismatch, failed repair, and writes failing before bytes, after a full record or midway through one. Delta checkpoints from upstream `3ae8e4405` now persist only unchanged-prefix additions, with full snapshots after edits or compaction. Run-created histories beyond 1,000 messages are chunked; an incomplete chunk sequence blocks continuation. Legacy snapshots remain readable. Seeded reconstruction, disk-size, write-failure and loop tests cover this integration. Automatic continuation of torn-tail journals is not included; existing recovery/review restrictions remain in force. Journal retirement also checks corruption independently of terminal status: a transcript acknowledgement never authorizes deleting a damaged tail or incomplete checkpoint.

Provider recovery: the bounded idle-stall rule from original StarNet `d985f2c69` is adapted to Moe's existing recovery policy. Two consecutive idle-watchdog failures on one provider/model take an already configured fallback or terminate with `provider_stalled`; cancellation and recovery budgets remain authoritative. Other failures, truncated responses and fallback switches reset the streak. Tests exercise the real adapter/watchdog with offline streams, including a stalled fallback. Host-injected retry jitter also spreads same-provider retries across agents, while preserving the original total local patience and per-wait cap. Server Retry-After delays are never shortened below the existing cap, even when local patience is nearly spent. Both ordinary and truncated-stream retry waits pass the live run signal to the existing provider abortable timer, so cancellation clears the timer/listener and prevents another model request. Safe host-read tool retries reuse that same abortable timer and preserve the original failure on cancellation; mutation and connector retries remain prohibited. These changes do not alter the adapter's pre-stream retry-exhaustion limit or claim live provider health.

Voice readiness: the original `voiceRouteAvailable` probe from upstream `079f23302` is wired into the existing voice tool's per-run description. Only a definite missing route produces a setup warning; unknown status does not invent an outage. The same provider list and Edge switch drive synthesis. A configured route is not evidence of network health, remaining credit or successful audio generation. Configuration changes remain effective for normal execution, with existing consent and workspace protections intact.

Reuse progress: Moe ports upstream `7bab4b448` into the existing web and owned-browser paths. Resolver failures, empty/malformed answers and mixed public/private results are rejected. Direct web fetch/request connections pin the selected IP per hop using the locked Undici runtime dependency, including Node lookup callbacks requesting all addresses; the URL retains Host/TLS identity. Owned Chromium uses the upstream loopback proxy for proxied HTTP(S)/WebSocket traffic, including subresources. Explicit local-development origins remain available through `browser.test_navigate`. Attached user browsers are not reconfigured and are outside this proxy protection. Existing desktop staging includes the declared production dependency. Unit tests and real local proxy connections exercise rejection and pinning; the full Chromium gauntlet requires an installed browser and is not claimed as locally verified when skipped. Existing Moe fail-closed tests are retained.

Runtime faults and red gates still take priority. Otherwise choose useful end-to-end results and reusable capabilities according to operator needs. ## Provider constraints checked during this audit

- YouTube has an upload API; uploads from certain unverified API projects are restricted to private until an audit. Direct integration therefore needs more than an API key. [YouTube videos.insert](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/videos/insert)
- Monetization requires original/authentic value; repetitive mass-produced content can be ineligible. Quality and monetization cannot be guaranteed by choosing an AI model. [YouTube monetization policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)
- Shotstack provides JSON-based asynchronous video rendering. A submission response must be followed by verified job completion. [Shotstack API](https://shotstack.io/docs/api/)
- Zernio exposes publishing tools through its hosted MCP connection. Actual account scopes and successful tool calls must still be verified in Moe. [Zernio MCP](https://docs.zernio.com/mcp)
- Etsy private operations require OAuth; access tokens expire after one hour and refresh tokens support renewal. Implement through the existing credentials boundary without exposing secrets in artifacts. [Etsy authentication](https://developer.etsy.com/documentation/essentials/authentication/)
- Paper trading is available through broker APIs, for example Alpaca. This is feasibility evidence, not broker selection or a claim of eligibility for this operator. [Paper-trading documentation](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/v1.4.2/docs/paper-trading)

## Evidence and operating boundaries

Keep configuration, catalogue discovery, credential connection, mock/unit tests, real provider acceptance and unattended acceptance distinct. CI cannot prove a video was produced, a shop sold something or a strategy made money. Never promote a domain to operational status without evidence from its actual execution path. Use existing consent, capability gates, daily budgets, audit, result envelopes and recovery. Preserve failed/uncertain states and resume by reconciliation rather than repeating side effects. Account setup or a material authorization gap is reported once as a concrete required action; ordinary engineering continues independently.

### Platform and legal constraints that change the design

- **Fiverr:** AI assistance is allowed, but work must be meaningfully refined and customized; generic unmodified AI output is not enough. Respect an explicit non-AI client requirement. Ordinary completed orders credit the seller 80% of purchase value before taxes; this is not net profit. Start with locally prepared gig copy, samples, intake and delivery files. No general account automation/API entitlement was verified: use an operator handoff until an authorized integration is established; no scraping, bot messaging or browser workaround. [AI guidance](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/37333301560593-Using-AI-on-Fiverr-Guidelines-for-freelancers-and-clients), [earnings](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/9234443621137-Your-earnings-page), [terms](https://www.fiverr.com/legal-portal/legal-terms/terms-of-service).
- **Etsy:** seller-prompted AI art requires creative involvement and disclosure. The recent official AI update explicitly rejects bot-generated listings without human involvement and AI-generated functional guides such as patterns/recipes. Do not plan an unattended listing factory or an AI instruction-PDF business. Policy-check both the item and its images before export/publication; production-partner requirements also need review for POD. Listing, transaction, country-specific processing, advertising and applicable tax costs must all enter estimates. Current fee basics include $0.20 listing and 6.5% transaction fees; they are not the total fee burden. [Creativity standards](https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity/), [AI update](https://www.etsy.com/uk/seller-handbook/article/1522733284810), [fee basics](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035902374-Etsy-Fee-Basics).
- **YouTube:** mass-produced/repetitive content can fail monetization rules. Ad-revenue YPP eligibility includes 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 qualified watch hours in 12 months or 10 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days, with further requirements and review; reaching thresholds does not guarantee acceptance. Unverified API projects can have uploads restricted to private. Add original editorial value, rights, applicable synthetic-media disclosures, caption/audio QC, upload reconciliation and real analytics rather than bulk generation. [Monetization policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392), [eligibility](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851), [upload API](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/videos/insert).
- **Social:** treat each platform as a separate capability/account-policy matrix. Meta's official Instagram collection documents professional accounts and publishing permissions; login variants have different prerequisites. The direct current content-publishing page was unavailable during this audit, so account types/scopes/limits must be revalidated during adapter work. No blanket claim of automated posting to every network. Prefer approved APIs/existing connectors; manual draft export remains available when an adapter is unsupported. [Meta-owned API collection](https://www.postman.com/meta/instagram/documentation/6yqw8pt/instagram-api).
- **German local outreach assumption:** business contact details being public does not grant marketing permission. Under UWG section 7, B2B calls require at least presumed consent; electronic advertising generally requires prior express consent, with a narrow existing-customer exception. The product must not infer consent from an old website. Prepare a draft and consent/basis review for the operator; automatic emails/calls remain disabled without separately established authorization and legal basis. Store minimal business data, source/time, suppression status and retention/deletion controls; assess GDPR obligations for personal contact data before use. [UWG section 7](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/__7.html).
- **Trading:** ESMA's historical 2018 CFD analysis found 74–89% of retail accounts typically lost money. This is dated CFD evidence, not a current percentage for all trading; it directly contradicts treating leveraged speculation as dependable passive income. No return promise, broker selection, deposits or live orders from this research. [ESMA evidence](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-agrees-prohibit-binary-options-and-restrict-cfds-protect-retail-investors).

### Bots learning from mistakes and operator explanations

Extend existing `decision.record` / `decision.outcome`, `sidecar/skills/library/decision-learning.md`, `sidecar/memory-store.js`, `frontend/app/memorydecisions.js` and skill version/evaluation mechanisms. Existing storage/review primitives do not establish that domain-specific learning is complete.

- On a failed delivery, repeated correction or material uncertainty, record expected versus observed result, run/artifact IDs, evidence, error class and what remains unknown. Separate tool outage, misunderstood brief, factual mistake, style preference and policy refusal; a refusal is not a failure to train away.
- Ask one short contextual question only when the answer changes the next action: for example, “Soll das Produkt farbgetreu bleiben, oder war eine stilisierte Werbeillustration gemeint?” Offer a corrected example and optional explanation; defer/skip is valid. Do not repeatedly ask answered questions or block unrelated work.
- Stage an editable lesson with scope (this customer, project or global), source, confidence, version, expiry/recheck date and a positive/negative example. Keep operator preferences separate from sourced facts. Do not store private customer assets or secrets as general knowledge; honor deletion and tenant separation.
- Test the proposed change on the original failure plus held-out examples, including conflicting customers and adversarial feedback. Only a valid existing review/policy decision promotes it. Conflicting evidence reopens review; kept/discarded history cannot be silently overwritten. Roll back regressions and show which lesson affected a later result.
- Refresh knowledge when a task needs it or a stored source becomes stale, within existing scheduled budgets. Source updates produce reviewable changes; no continuous scraping/model-training loop, self-granted permissions or automatic production-code rewriting. This is controlled memory/workflow improvement, not a claim of model-weight training.
- Acceptance: fail → operator correction → pending lesson → review → cold restart → scoped reuse → measurable correction; reject unapproved/global leakage, stale/conflicting lesson, prompt injection and permission escalation; demonstrate rollback and deletion. Surface useful before/after evidence in the existing UI.

Delivery order: BIZ-1 and the BIZ-5 lesson contract first, then one complete BIZ-2 or BIZ-3 flow, followed by BIZ-4 adapters. Keep broader roadmap work and mandatory safety/recovery ahead of live operation. First visible milestone should be a reusable offer/sample/economics bundle, not another catalogue of unsupported capabilities. Research does not authorize outreach, paid generation, account creation, publication or trading.
