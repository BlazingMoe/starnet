# Moe: life and business automation

Recorded 2026-10-02 from the operator's explicit product direction. This is a living delivery plan, not evidence that accounts are connected or businesses are running.

## Product goal

Moe is the operator's versatile central personal system for everyday life, learning, research, creative work, software, documents, data, communication, planning and business. The operator clarified that the following three jobs are examples of the breadth desired, not a restriction to three industries or a fixed delivery sequence:

- A YouTube channel that researches an audience, produces original useful videos, edits them, publishes them and learns from real results with minimal routine intervention.
- An Etsy operation that researches demand and target audiences, develops eligible products, prepares and manages listings, fulfils orders and reconciles results.
- Trading through a supported broker with explicit strategy, exposure limits, execution reconciliation and emergency stop, initially in simulation.

The general product requirement is a common goal-to-result execution path for varied assignments: understand, plan, choose existing tools and skills, execute, persist, recover, verify and deliver. Extend domains through compatible integrations and reusable recipes on the same runtime. Evaluate new work by actual need and usefulness across domains as well as complete outcomes. Support depends on available tools, account access and permissions; the examples do not imply universal capability.

Use scoped standing authorizations to remove repetitive approvals where the existing policy supports them. Initial account consent, policy setup and exceptional decisions remain necessary. This product direction does not itself authorize live trades, public uploads, customer messages, purchases or other financial commitments. No promise of revenue, investment returns or permanent operation without supervision is made.

## Example delivery paths and acceptance

Reuse progress: Moe now ports the fail-closed DNS validation portion of upstream `7bab4b448` into the existing web tools. Resolver failures, empty/malformed answers and mixed public/private results are rejected before network access, including redirect validation. Regression tests exercise both `web_fetch` and `web_request`. Binding the actual socket to the validated IP (and the browser proxy changes) remains a separate dependency-aware follow-up; this partial port does not claim to close DNS rebinding completely.

Runtime faults and red gates still take priority. Otherwise choose useful end-to-end results and reusable capabilities according to operator needs. The paths below describe dependencies within illustrative domains; their numbering does not mandate finishing these domains before other personal-system work.

1. **YouTube preparation — recipe available, generated output not yet acceptance-tested.** `youtube-production-pack` uses the existing catalogue, launch form, filesystem tools and research/review skills. It reads real archives and prior packs, researches an audience need and requests a production folder with sourced brief, script, edit plan, asset rights ledger, metadata and readiness report. It neither renders nor publishes. Its instructions are workflow guidance; runtime permissions remain the enforcement authority. A successful real run must save and read back the files, retain source references and report missing rights/assets honestly.
2. **YouTube render — possible next milestone within this example.** First inspect the available Shotstack/generation/voice routes and original tools. Reuse the existing request/connector execution boundary. Persist submission intent and provider render ID through existing task artifacts/journal; never resubmit an ambiguous paid request. Poll through existing scheduling infrastructure, verify downloaded output and retain provider/cost evidence. Test failure, timeout, crash and cancellation with fakes before a bounded authorized real render. Required evidence: playable video, accurate narration/captions, usable audio, rights evidence and a truthful quality verdict. A timeline draft alone is not a finished video.
3. **YouTube publish and feedback.** Use a connected existing publishing route where compatible. Validate account identity, channel, privacy and content policy before the action. Test with private uploads first; reconcile remote IDs after interruption and prevent duplicate posts. Public scheduling needs a scoped publishing grant, budget, E-STOP and an explicit quality policy. Track real watch/retention data when available; never fabricate analytics or treat views as profit. Routine success should be quiet; blocked or ambiguous actions should surface.
4. **Etsy research to draft, then operation.** Reuse opportunity research and POD APIs. Save source-backed demand hypotheses, audience segments, competitor differentiation, rights evidence, product/fee/shipping costs and uncertainty. Public estimates do not prove which products sell most. Add managed OAuth refresh and account health before unattended writes. Progress from draft listing to an authorized test listing/order, then reconciliation of inventory, fulfilment, cancellations/refunds and margins. Test expired credentials and unknown remote outcomes. Do not work around token expiry with browser sessions or raw credentials in prompts.
5. **Trading simulation, then separately authorized live execution.** First choose a compatible broker based on actual account access and supported markets; do not silently choose one. Start with paper trading, distinct credentials/endpoints, position/order reconciliation, stale quote rejection, duplicate-order prevention and bounded exposure. Paper results must include assumptions on fees/slippage and are not proof of live performance. Live mode requires separate authorization and enforceable per-order, position and daily-loss limits. It must stop safely on E-STOP, lost account state or unresolved order status.

## Provider constraints checked during this audit

- YouTube has an upload API; uploads from certain unverified API projects are restricted to private until an audit. Direct integration therefore needs more than an API key. [YouTube videos.insert](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/videos/insert)
- Monetization requires original/authentic value; repetitive mass-produced content can be ineligible. Quality and monetization cannot be guaranteed by choosing an AI model. [YouTube monetization policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)
- Shotstack provides JSON-based asynchronous video rendering. A submission response must be followed by verified job completion. [Shotstack API](https://shotstack.io/docs/api/)
- Zernio exposes publishing tools through its hosted MCP connection. Actual account scopes and successful tool calls must still be verified in Moe. [Zernio MCP](https://docs.zernio.com/mcp)
- Etsy private operations require OAuth; access tokens expire after one hour and refresh tokens support renewal. Implement through the existing credentials boundary without exposing secrets in artifacts. [Etsy authentication](https://developer.etsy.com/documentation/essentials/authentication/)
- Paper trading is available through broker APIs, for example Alpaca. This is feasibility evidence, not broker selection or a claim of eligibility for this operator. [Paper-trading documentation](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/v1.4.2/docs/paper-trading)

## Evidence and operating boundaries

Keep configuration, catalogue discovery, credential connection, mock/unit tests, real provider acceptance and unattended acceptance distinct. CI cannot prove a video was produced, a shop sold something or a strategy made money. Never promote a domain to operational status without evidence from its actual execution path. Use existing consent, capability gates, daily budgets, audit, result envelopes and recovery. Preserve failed/uncertain states and resume by reconciliation rather than repeating side effects. Account setup or a material authorization gap is reported once as a concrete required action; ordinary engineering continues independently.
