## Reusable operating method

The useful method is independent of the promotional income claims:

1. Select a narrow service using dated, attributable demand evidence.
2. Decompose it into typed roles and handoffs.
3. Route hard management/review decisions to the strongest available model and bounded repetitive steps to cheaper workers.
4. Persist private drafts, receipts, versions and economics before any external action.
5. Require human quality, rights and policy review; improve with measured outcomes.
6. Schedule or automate only after repeated, reconciled success and separate authorization.

The videos repeatedly encourage copying successful designs or mass automation. Moe AI Station deliberately rejects that part: competitor evidence may identify a category, audience or quality bar, but outputs must be original or licensed, customized, human-reviewed and attributable. Research, preparation and private export never imply permission to scrape, message, list, publish, fulfil, charge or trade.
